# NB0507-14 GC × coverage × Kraken2 figure caption

Original full-read assembly (A) and nominal 25× sampled-read reassembly (B) of the old Buck NB0507-14 run. Each point represents one contig, positioned by GC percentage and self-mapping read coverage on identical logarithmic coverage axes; the inset expands 0.5–10× in panel B. Colors show provisional broad Kraken2 contig assignments. Panels contain 72,565 contigs/46.41 Mb and 6,113 contigs/8.01 Mb, respectively. Zero-coverage contigs (410 and 18) are omitted from the scatterplots but included in the assembly totals. Both read sets were mapped to their own assemblies. The nominal 25× target reflects sampled read bases per assumed genome, not mapped coverage. Composition shifts do not establish contaminant removal or species identity.
