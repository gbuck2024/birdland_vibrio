  TRAILING:3 \
  SLIDINGWINDOW:4:20 \
  MINLEN:50

Rationale
- ILLUMINACLIP removes standard TruSeq paired-end adapter sequence.
- LEADING and TRAILING remove low-quality terminal bases without aggressive front trimming.
- SLIDINGWINDOW:4:20 is intended to reduce poor-quality tail sequence, including reverse-read tail artifacts seen in FastQC.
- MINLEN:50 preserves bacterial short-read usability while discarding excessively short fragments.
